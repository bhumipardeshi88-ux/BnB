BalSetu — Bridge for Children
BalSetu connects children in orphanages with families who truly want them, and with volunteers and donors who support their education and well-being. The name means "Bridge for Children" in Hindi.

Problem Statement :
Orphaned children often get placed with families that aren't truly ready ; and when it doesn't work out, they're returned. Each return is another rejection for a child who's already lost their family. Every child deserves a family that genuinely wants them, and stays.

The Safety Gap
Most platforms share children's personal details like photos, names, profiles that help match families. This puts kids at risk of trafficking and exploitation. A child should never have their identity exposed in order to find a home.

Our Solution
BalSetu helps children find families without exposing any personal data. Instead of individual child profiles, families see only age-range blocks with counts (e.g., "6–9, 3 children, speak Hindi") — enough to know if there's a fit, but nothing that identifies a child.

Every appointment is verified through the official government portal, so families are checked before they ever meet a child. This dramatically reduces the risk of trafficking and ensures children only go to families that have been properly vetted. The child's identity stays protected until a real match is confirmed.

Features
Adopt: Children are shown through age-range blocks (Under 2, 2–5, 6–9, 10–12, 13–17, 18+) with counts and no individual profiles therefore, no child is exposed.
Book an Appointment: Families request a visit; the government portal verifies them before the orphanage contacts them directly.
Volunteer: Children get access to volunteers who teach, mentor, give talks, and more ; including a custom "my own topic" option.
Donate: Support a child's education, supplies, meals, or general care.
Benefits: Volunteers unlock certificates, LORs, verified hours, and badges after their first session.
Credits & Referrals: Volunteers earn credits for volunteering and referring friends.
